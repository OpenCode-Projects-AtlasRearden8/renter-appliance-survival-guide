# The Renter Appliance Survival Guide

*A friendly manual for the appliances you live with — written for people who have never opened a toolbox on purpose.*

Welcome. This guide exists because appliance manuals are written for lawyers and installers, not for the rest of us. If your dishwasher is blinking something cryptic at 10 p.m., you don't want a service manual — you want a friend who knows a bit about appliances and talks like a normal person. That's this guide.

## The one-paragraph version of this whole guide

Most appliance "failures" are actually: something dirty, something blocked, or a confused computer. Clean the filter, clear the drain, cut power for 60 seconds, and a surprising number of problems just go away. Learn the small monthly habits, and you'll avoid the expensive calls — and when a call *is* needed, this guide tells you how to have it without getting talked into a junk appliance.

## Honesty disclaimer

This guide is **general education, not a repair manual for your specific model**. It never replaces the owner's manual, manufacturer instructions, or a licensed technician. If anything smells like smoke, gas, or burning plastic — stop, unplug it, and read the [call-a-pro checklist](call-a-pro-checklist.html).
